Offgrid Bookshelf Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Offgrid Bookshelf is built to keep your audiobook and ebook library on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses, stores, and shares.

Information the app accesses

Offgrid Bookshelf can access audio files, ebook files, image files, and folders that you choose through Android's file picker. The app uses those files to import books, attach ebooks, display cover images, play audio, and export selected books.

The app may also use notification permission so playback controls and playback status can appear while audio is playing.

Information stored on your device

The app stores library information locally on your device, including book titles, file locations, playback position, chapters, cover paths, ratings, and app settings.

Imported files may be copied into the app's local storage when you import files or choose "Save to device."

Information sent outside the app

Offgrid Bookshelf does not sell your data and does not provide an account system.

Most library activity stays on your device. If you use cover search, the search terms you enter may be sent to Open Library so cover results can be shown. If paid upgrades are added, purchases are handled by Google Play. Offgrid Bookshelf does not receive your card information.

Sharing

Offgrid Bookshelf does not share your audiobook library, playback history, or personal files with advertisers or data brokers.

Data deletion

You can remove books inside the app. You can also delete the app or clear its app data in Android settings to remove local library data stored by the app.

Children's privacy

Offgrid Bookshelf is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email listed on the app's Google Play store page.
